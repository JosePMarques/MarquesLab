About the lab

At MaRQuEs Lab, we work on quantitative magnetic resonance imaging (qMRI): techniques that turn MRI signals into meaningful, reproducible measurements of tissue properties.

Our group is part of the MR Structural & Quantitative Imaging unit at the Donders Centre for Cognitive Neuroimaging, Radboud University in Nijmegen, The Netherlands.

From acquisition and signal modelling to open tools and clinical research, we aim to make quantitative imaging more reliable and useful for understanding the human brain.

What we work on

Research

Connecting MRI physics with robust measurements of brain tissue.

01 / ACQUISITION

Quantitative MRI

Designing and validating MRI methods that measure tissue properties, including T₁ and T₂*.

02 / BIOLOGY

Brain tissue & myelin

Studying tissue microstructure and developing imaging approaches to probe myelin and iron.

03 / TRANSLATION

Methods for research

Building reproducible, practical methods that connect MRI physics to neuroscience and clinical questions.
